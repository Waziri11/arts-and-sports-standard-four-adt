How to play:

1.

Exercise to prepare your body by running slowly for 2 to 3 minutes;

2.

Mark the starting and ending points of the playing area.

The distance can be between 25, 50, 75, or 100 meters depending on the ability of the players;

3.

Put off your shoes and hold your racing sack;

4.

Stand at the starting point;

5.

Stand inside the sack and make sure it goes beyond the waist;

6.

Hold the sack with your hands so it doesn’t fall;

7.

Wait instructions from the leader;

8.

After the command from the leader, start jumping forward with two legs together;

9.

Run faster than others towards the finish line, as shown in Figure 1; and

10.

The winner is the one who will be the first to cross the finishing line.
