2.

Select a song for the show.

You are advised to choose a song that you like.

The song should also have an attractive message that reflects people’s lives in the community you come from;

3.

Have enough time to rehearse.

The rehearsal should involve vocal and singing exercises.

If the song is to be sung by a group, practise with your fellow singers.

Good preparation makes the singer confident.

Therefore, he or she can perform well in front of the audience;

4.

Before the performance, arrive early at the performance venue or platform that will be used and do some rehearsals.

The rehearsing includes knowing the stage and testing the musical instruments;

5.

On the day of the performance, have enough time to rest before the performance time.

Also arrive at performance area on time;

6.

Be confident with the preparations you have made and avoid showing signs of wanting favours that could lead you into corrupt practices; and

7.

During the performance, express the feelings you have about the song confidently as you send the intended message to the audience.

If it is a happy event, smile and show your happiness.

If it is a sad event, express the mood of sadness with the relevant voice or actions.
