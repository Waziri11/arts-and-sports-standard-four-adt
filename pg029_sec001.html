Steps of drawing a human stick figure

(i)

Think of the direction of the human form.

Choose whether the figure will face to the right or left;

(ii)

Draw a circle for the head.

Start with a small circle at the top of your paper.

This will be the head of your human stick figure;

(iii)

Draw the neck and shoulders.

Draw a cross line connecting with the head;

(iv)

Draw the torso (chest, stomach and waist).

Draw a straight vertical line down from the head.

This line will be the main body of your human stick figure;

(v)

Draw the arms.

Draw two straight lines extending from the upper part of the body line.

These lines represent the arms.

You can add small circles at the ends for palms;

(vi)

Draw the legs.

Draw two straight lines extending downwards branching from the bottom of the cross.

These lines indicate the legs.

You can add small circles at the ends for feet; and

(vii)

Bold the lines.

Bold the lines of your image with a pencil, as shown in Figure 2.
