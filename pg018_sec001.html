Singing with correct diction

Learning correct diction is necessary for a good singer.

A singer should consider the correct diction to convey the intended message.

Important issues to consider for correct diction during singing include:

(a)

Opening the mouth properly while singing.

Proper mouth opening helps in the correct diction of vowels and consonants.

Therefore, a singer should pronounce words correctly to ensure the audience understands them;

(b)

Breathing correctly while singing.

A singer should inhale slowly and deeply, then exhale in a controlled manner to manage airflow.

This helps to avoid a shortage of breath while singing;
