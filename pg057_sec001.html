How to play:

1.

Prepare a healthy chicken.

It can be a cock or a hen;

2.

Stay into two teams.

Each should have no more than four players depending on the size of the field;

3.

Non-playing pupils should form a circle so that the chicken can run around but does not escape;

4.

The leader will start the game by throwing the chicken in the center of the circle, then blowing the whistle to start the game;

and

5.

After the command of the leader, chase the chicken until you catch it, as shown in Figure 2.
