Modern games

Modern games are games which originated from other communities outside Tanzania.

These games began to be played in other areas such as Asia and Europe.

Modern games are much famous in the world.

There are many modern games, some of them include acrobatics, football and netball.

Acrobatics game

Acrobatics is a type of game or activity that involves displaying physical skills, bravery, and various exercises that are performed in front of an audience.

In acrobatics, participants can perform various fun or amazing movements such as jumping, balancing, playing with various equipment and creating various shapes on land and in the air.

Acrobatics are often performed to entertain the audience.
