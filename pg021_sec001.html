Singing in two-part harmony

Singing in two-part harmony involves two singers or two groups singing different sounds at the same time.

These two voices are sung together while maintaining harmony.

Singing in two-part harmony requires great attention and enough skill.

Two singers singing in two-part harmony must work together equally.

At this stage, you will learn the second voice (alto) to master singing in two-part harmony.

The second voice contributes to bringing a harmonious balance when singing.

The second voice is lower than the first voice.

When singing in two-part harmony, the singer must consider correct diction and tone to make the song attractive to the audience.
