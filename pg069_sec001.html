Fine art exhibitions
This kind of exhibition at the school level aims to enable a pupil to show the creative works of fine art done for a certain period, for example, a month or a year depending on the type of exhibition.
Also, they aim to encourage pupils to participate more in drawing and clay modelling to develop their talents.
Likewise, teachers, parents and society in general get a chance to see their children’s academic progress.
To participate in fine art exhibitions, you should consider the following steps:
1.
Choose a display topic that will go with your artwork.
Choosing a topic that reflects life in the community you come from is good.
2.
Make preparations together with your fellow pupils or performers to come up with appropriate methods for preparing an attractive presentation.
3.
Prepare a short timetable that will show the search of the exhibition area, such as at the playground, under the tree, the identification and preparation of the artworks to be exhibited, the arrangement and placement of artworks within the exhibition area, and the date when the exhibition will take place.
4.
Prepare the materials or tools to be used for setting up an exhibition area.
