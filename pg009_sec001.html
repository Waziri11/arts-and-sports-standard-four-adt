Using body movements to act out living and non-living things

Body movements can be used to imitate the behaviour, motions and emotions of living and non-living things through actors’ bodies.

To imitate living and non-living things using body movements, it is better to consider the following issues:

(a)

Observing the behaviour of the character you want to imitate to see how the character walks, sits, eats, or even defends oneself.

You can use ICT to find videos or pictures of the characters and carefully observe their movements;

(b)

Understanding the body of the character you want to act.

It is necessary to observe the body structure of the character.

If you imitate an insect consider the number of legs, wings, and how those parts relate to each other.

Also, it is good to consider the weight of the character imitated such as how heavy or light the character is, because weight can affect how the character moves;
