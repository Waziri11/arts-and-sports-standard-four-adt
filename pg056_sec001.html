Practise the game of the sack race
The importance of the sack race game

This game has the following benefits for players:

  • (a) It entertains the participants and audiences;
  • (b) It builds friendship and teamwork;
  • (c) It improves the ability to think;
  • (d) Energizes participants’ bodies;
  • (e) It develops the competitive spirit;
  • (f) It promotes their health; and
  • (g) It nurtures the running talents.
Exercise 1

Chicken chase game

This is a traditional game and very popular in some places; often in children’s bonanzas or sports concerts. It is a game that can be played by people of all ages. This game aims to have fun, improve health, and build teamwork among the players. It is important to ensure that the chicken that will be used will not be harmed or hurt while playing this game.
