(b)

If the chicken goes out of the circle without being caught by any player, the game is repeated;

(c)

The time to chase the chicken is between three to five minutes, if the chicken is not caught, players from other teams will enter;

(d)

It is not allowed to kick the chicken to catch it;

(e)

It is not allowed to step on the chicken to catch it; and

(f)

If you are not a player, you are not allowed to catch the chicken even if it passes near you.

Importance of chicken chase game

The following are the benefits of this game to players:

(a)

It makes their bodies active;

(b)

It builds friendship and teamwork;

(c)

It improves the ability to think;

(d)

It provides the body with physical exercises;

(e)

It develops the competitive spirit; and

(f)

It nurtures the running talents.
