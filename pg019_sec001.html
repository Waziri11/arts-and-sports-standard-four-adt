(c) Maintaining tempo of the song.

A singer should sing at the correct speed to ensure that words are pronounced at the right time, neither too fast nor too slow; and

(d) Understanding the meaning of words.

When a singer knows the meaning of the words, he or she can pronounce them with appropriate emotions, according to the context.

This makes the performance more attractive for the audience.
