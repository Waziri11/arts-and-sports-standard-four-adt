Participating in performing arts

To participate in performing arts, consider the following steps:

1.

Choose a topic for the performance.

It is good to choose a message that reflects the daily life of your community.

You can discuss with your teacher to choose the relevant topic.

Examples of messages that can be used are effects of corruption, diseases, environment, tax education, children’s rights, or the Union of Tanganyika and Zanzibar;

2.

Stay in groups with your fellows for preparations of rehearsals and performances;

3.

Prepare timetable for the performance.

Make sure the timetable indicates preparation, practices and performance schedule;

4.

Rehearse based on the instructions from the teacher.

Make sure that you rehearse enough to have a good performance;

5.

Prepare the tools needed for the performance.

These include costumes, make-up, and props, depending on the performance;

6.

As the day of the performance reaches, rehearse in the area that will be used on the day of the performance;

7.

On the performance day, make sure everything is well prepared, especially the stage and the props;

8.

In case you are using electrical devices, make sure they are tested in advance to see if they are safe and working properly.
