Introduction

This textbook, Arts and Sports is written specifically for Standard Four Pupils in mainland Tanzania.

This book has been prepared according to the Arts and Sports syllabus issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology in 2023.

Some of the themes in this book have been taken from the previous book Vocational Skills Standard Five published in 2018 by the 2016 syllabi.

This book has seven chapters, which are Acting, Singing and musical instruments, Drawing, Clay modelling, Physical exercises, Traditional and modern games, Art exhibitions, performances and sports competitions.

By learning its contents, you will master the basics of singing and acting, drawing pictures, and modelling various shapes.

You will also manage physical exercises, play traditional and modern games, and participate in arts exhibitions, performances and sports competitions.

The contents of these chapters are presented in the form of text, activities, interesting pictures, and exercises.

Therefore, perform all the tasks in this book and other tasks that the teacher will provide.

This will enable you to develop the knowledge and skills intended.
