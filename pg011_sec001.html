Activity 2

Use body movements and voice to act the movements of a cat, monkey, chicken and lion.

Examples of acting movements of non-living things

FOR ONLINE READING ONLY

1. Acting the movements of a bicycle. Using your hands and feet, hold your hands in front of you as if you are holding the handlebars of a bicycle. Bend your knees slightly and start moving your feet as if you are pedaling. Using your body, lean slightly forward and turn your body side to side as if you are steering a bicycle;

2. Acting the movements of a car. Hold your hands in front of you as if you are holding a car steering wheel. Turn the steering wheel with your hands as if you are steering a car. Use one foot as if pressing the gas pedal and the other for the brake pedal;

3. Acting the movements of a motorcycle. Hold your hands in front of you as if you are holding the handlebars of a motorcycle, then using your feet, kick as if you are starting the motorcycle. Turn the handlebars with your hands and start rotating your hands as if you are steering a motorcycle. Lean slightly forward and turn your body side to side as if you are steering a motorcycle; and

4. Acting the movements of the stone. Hold your hand as if you have held a stone of a size that can be moved using your legs. Put it down and use your leg to make it move from one point to another.
