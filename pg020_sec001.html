Singing by considering tone

Tone is a sound with a specific pitch and quality that comes from a musical note or a person’s voice.

In singing, it is important to consider the correct tone for each word and sentence.

For a song to have the desired quality, each word should be sung with a meaningful tone that conveys the emotions of the song.

Proper use of tone enhances the beauty and quality of the voice.

Therefore, a singer should practise singing while considering the tone, ensuring he or she delivers the song skillfully and emotionally.

There are various aspects that a singer should consider to produce a high-quality voice.

These aspects include:

(a)

Correct posture when singing.

A singer should make sure he or she keeps the body balanced, with the neck upright, shoulders and arms relaxed, chest and stomach not tense, and feet slightly apart;

(b)

Proper breathing while singing.

To breathe correctly when singing, a singer needs to practise breathing exercises.

For example, taking a deep breath, holding it for a moment, and then slowly releasing it; and

(c)

Correct diction while singing.

Proper pronunciation is important for achieving the correct tone when singing.

Also, it helps a singer to produce a clear and pure sound.

In addition, it improves the quality of the voice.

This creates a tone that appeals to listeners and ensures the message of the song is understood easily.
