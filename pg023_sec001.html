Arrangement of singers and musical instrument players

Singers and musical instrument players have different ways of arranging themselves on stage.

They can arrange themselves based on the number of voices singing and the type and number of musical instruments being used.

The goal is to achieve good quality and balance between the singer’s voices and the music from the instruments.

To have a good arrangement, the following things can be considered:

(a)

The soprano singers must be together;

(b)

The alto singers must be together;

(c)

Musical instruments players of the same type should be together; and

(d)

Low-pitched musical instrument players should not stay next to high-pitched ones, as shown in Figure 1.

This is because if you mix low-pitched and high-pitched instruments, the low-pitched ones will not be well heard.
